# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme projects follow a lifecycle of **Initiation, Planning, Execution, Release, and Retrospective/Continuous Improvement**. Teams confirm the problem, stakeholders, success measures, and initial risks during initiation, then turn approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, milestones, and a Definition of Done.

During execution, teams track work on a project board (Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review, QA, Done) and deliver in small increments. Pull requests should link issues and acceptance criteria, pass automated tests and linting in CI, and receive at least one approval before merge (or follow team policy). Unit and integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, security scanning, and manual QA where needed support quality assurance.

Project Managers coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize work, and measure success; Developers build, test, and review; and QA/Testing contributors validate acceptance criteria. Teams use standups, weekly delivery and PM/PdM syncs, milestone demos, and stakeholder updates to share progress and blockers. Risks and dependencies are recorded with owners and mitigations, reviewed regularly, and escalated from the team through the PM and Product Lead to the sponsor when needed. Release readiness includes met acceptance criteria, passing CI and security scans, release notes, a rollback plan, staging smoke tests, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives capture learnings and follow-up actions for continuous improvement.
